# Judges' Demo Script (≈3 minutes)

Centerpiece: **Senior protection** and **Youth safety**. All examples are fictional and labeled DEMO EXAMPLE.

## 0:00 — The problem (20 s)
"AI makes scams more convincing than ever — perfect grammar, fake recruiters, fake agencies, fake 'friends'.
The people most at risk — seniors and kids — often have no one to ask *in the moment*. TruthArmor is that moment:
**Pause. Verify. Protect.**"

## 0:20 — Home screen (15 s)
Show the calm navy/white design, "What would you like to check?", **Quick Scan**, the Seniors / Youth / Families
cards, and the category grid. Mention **Simple Mode** for seniors (toggle it in Settings to show bigger text).

## 0:35 — Senior protection: government "arrest" call (50 s)
Home → *Try a demo example* → **Government "arrest" threat**.
- Point out **HIGH RISK** and the four-color meter.
- *Why this was flagged:* gift cards, arrest threat, "don't tell anyone", urgency.
- *What to do next* → tap **How to verify safely**: "Do not use the contact information in the suspicious
  message."
- Show **Where to get help** (TIGTA, Elder Fraud Hotline).
- Open **How was this calculated?** — "Rules have fixed weights; gift cards plus a claimed agency sets a minimum
  of 85. The AI can only nudge the score, so it can't talk itself out of a clear scam."

## 1:25 — Youth safety: a gaming "friend" (50 s)
Demo examples → **Concerning youth conversation**.
- The wording: "**Potentially unsafe interaction. Possible grooming indicators**" — never "predator".
- *What to do next:* involve a trusted adult, save (don't delete) the conversation, don't confront, report.
- Resources: NCMEC CyberTipline, Take It Down, Childhelp, 911.
- "We deliberately built **no secret monitoring**. It's about recognition and trusted adults."

## 2:15 — Real input + privacy (30 s)
Quick Scan → paste a message (or upload a screenshot on a phone) → TruthArmor suggests a category → confirm.
Show the OCR **review** step and the **sensitive-data warning**. Mention: screenshots never leave the phone,
numbers are redacted before AI, history stores only date/category/risk.

## 2:45 — Technical innovation + responsible AI (15 s)
"Hybrid engine: category engine + rules + OCR + AI + link reputation, all explainable. The AI key lives on a
secure server — never in the app. Every result shows confidence separately and ends with a clear disclaimer."

---

### Backup plan
If there's no internet, the app still works: rules, OCR, and scoring run on the device, and results note that AI
analysis was unavailable. In Demo mode, results are labeled **"DEMO AI — NOT LIVE AI"**.

### Questions judges may ask
- *"How do you know it's a scam?"* — We don't claim to. We identify known patterns, explain each one, and tell
  people how to verify independently.
- *"What if the AI is wrong?"* — It's one input. Fixed-weight rules and minimum scores for known dangerous
  combinations keep results stable; confidence is shown separately.
- *"What about privacy?"* — Minimum data, on-device OCR, redaction twice, metadata-only history, delete anytime.
